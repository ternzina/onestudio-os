## The resource editors can actually evaluate

- Completely free financial-education worksheet for comparing **2 or 3 written U.S. personal loan offers**.
- Fields for amount borrowed, cash received, disclosed APR and interest rate, fees, installment amounts, payment terms, early-payoff provisions and follow-up questions.
- Browser-print / Save as PDF; no account, email, SSN, uploads, or submission of typed numbers.
- Provides only an elementary equal-payment arithmetic check; does not calculate APR, guarantee eligibility, offer credit or rank lenders.
- Educational sources: [CFPB personal-loan fees](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/do-personal-installment-loans-have-fees-en-2120/) and [CFPB interest rate vs APR](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/what-is-the-difference-between-a-loan-interest-rate-and-the-apr-en-733/).
- **Commercial disclosure must always be stated honestly**: CashPath is an advertising-supported referral website, not a lender. The tool is free and can be used without requesting a loan.
